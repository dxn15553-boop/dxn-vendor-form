DXN COCOZHI

Product Name: DXN Cocozhi

Description:
500 gm powder form of coffee with cocoa powder (each sachet contains 25 gm).

Ingredients:
Non-Dairy Creamer [Glucose Syrup Solids, Vegetable Fat (palm), Dipotassium Hydrogen Phosphate, Sodium Tripolyphosphate, Sodium Caseinate (a milk derivative), Mono and Diglycerides of Fatty Acids, Diacetyl tartaric and Fatty Acid Esters of Glycerol, Beta Carotene], Sugar, Cocoa Powder, Skimmed Milk Powder, Malt Extract, Vanilla and Ganoderma Extract.

Directions for Use:
Pour the contents of Cocozhi in a cup, add 150 ml of hot water/milk and stir.

36 months

Powder
